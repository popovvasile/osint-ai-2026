Corporate OSINT Methodology

1\. Open source intelligence (OSINT)

- OSINT basics and its application in corporate analysis.

- Sources of information: open databases, social networks, forums, state
  registers.

- OSINT tools (Maltego, SpiderFoot, Recon-ng, etc.).

2\. OSINT methodology

- The main stages of information gathering are intelligence, analysis,
  and interpretation.

- Data collection methods: search queries, web page parsing, leakage
  analysis.

- Security and anonymity when working with OSINT.

3\. Business investigations

- Analysis of companies, their structure, and financial condition.

- Checking related organizations and partners.

- Identification of ultimate beneficiaries and key persons.

4\. Organization.

- Search for company registration data.

- Definition of legal entities and affiliates.

- Analysis of corporate documents and reports.

5\. Locations.

- Identify physical addresses of companies and branches.

- Analysis of offices and production facilities.

- Geospatial OSINT (Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, satellite images).

6\. 6. personnel.

- Search for employee information on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Twitter.

- Analysis of work experience and connections within the company.

- Assessment of the personnel structure and possible information leaks.

7\. Contact information

- Search for corporate contacts, email addresses, phone numbers.

- Analysis of public records and databases (Hunter.io, Email-Checker).

- Checking contacts for leaks.

8\. Business records

- Reviewing financial statements, balance sheets, and tax returns.

- Search for court decisions, arbitration cases and corporate disputes.

- Analysis of credit history and solvency.

9\. Services.

- Analysis of the services provided by the company.

- Checking for compliance with legal requirements and certifications.

- Search for reviews, evaluate the quality of services.

10\. Social networks

- Monitoring of company and employee social networks.

- Analyze customer activity, posts, and reactions.

- Identification of possible threats through social engineering.

11\. Information about domains

- Analysis of domains, WHOIS records, and IP addresses.

- Search for subdomains and related resources.

- Determining the organization\'s digital footprint.

12\. Public registers

- Use of open state registers.

- Check licenses, certificates, and permits.

- Analysis of patent data.

13\. Domain structure

- Researching the structure of corporate domains.

- Define internal and external subdomains.

- Identification of hosting providers.

14\. Cloud storage

- Search for information in cloud storage (Amazon S3, Google Drive,
  Dropbox).

- Analyzing data leaks from cloud services.

- Assessment of cloud infrastructure security.

15\. Email addresses

- Search and verify corporate email addresses.

- Verify email addresses in data leaks.

- Analysis of corporate address templates.

16\. 16. Third parties

- Analysis of contractors, partners, and suppliers.

- Checking for possible risks of cooperation.

- Assessment of the impact of third parties on business processes.

17\. Network connections

- Identify corporate networks and related infrastructures.

- Analysis of network connections and interactions.

- Identification of vulnerabilities in the company\'s network.

18\. Technologies used

- Researching the technologies and software used.

- Identify vulnerable components and versions.

- Analysis of the use of cloud services and CRM systems.

19\. Information leaks

- Search for corporate data in leaks.

- Analysis of compromised accounts.

- Assessing the impact of leaks on business.

20\. Archives

- Studying the company\'s historical data.

- Analysis of changes in the business model.

- Detection of deleted or hidden information.

21\. Internal leakage

- Checking insider threats.

- Analysis of information leaks from internal channels.

- Assessment of the consequences of potential leaks.

22\. Hacking.

- Researching the history of cyber incidents.

- Analysis of attacks on the company and related organizations.

- Evaluation of response and recovery measures.

23\. Analytics.

- Compiling a comprehensive company profile.

- Interpretation of the collected data.

- Developing strategies to protect against corporate OSINT.
